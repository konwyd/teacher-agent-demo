First, I am **creative**. I love painting and writing stories. I often use my imagination to create new things. For example, I designed a poster for our school's art festival last month, and my teacher praised me for my creative ideas.

Second, I am **hard-working**. I always try my best in everything I do. When I have difficulty with math problems, I don't give up easily. I keep practicing until I understand them well. My grades have improved a lot because of my hard work.

Third, I am **kind**. I enjoy helping others. When my classmates need help with their studies, I am always ready to share what I know. I also volunteer at the old people's home on weekends, bringing them joy and warmth.

These three words best describe who I am, and I will continue to be a better person.
