First, I am **creative**. I love painting and writing stories. When I have free time, I often draw pictures of nature or write short poems. My art teacher once said my paintings show new ideas. This makes me happy because I can express myself in different ways.

Second, I am **hard-working**. I always try my best in everything I do. For example, I spend two hours every day practicing English and math. Even when the subjects are difficult, I never give up. Last term, I got good grades because of my hard work.

Third, I am **kind**. I enjoy helping others. When my classmates have problems with their homework, I am always ready to explain. I also volunteer at the old people's home on weekends. Seeing their smiles makes me feel warm inside.
